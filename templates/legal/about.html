About RM Stock Tracker

A simple and structured way to track the U.S. stock market

RM Stock Tracker is a web app built to help users explore U.S. stocks and market indices in a clear and organized way. The focus is on simplicity, structure, and useful insights.

What You Can Do

  • Search and explore U.S. stocks and major market indices
  • View trending stocks and top market movers across the overall market and major indices such as the S&P 500, Nasdaq 100, and Dow Jones
  • View historical price data and key technical indicators
  • Use interactive charts to gain insights into stock performance
  • Organize stocks into folders using a structured watchlist
  • Customize your watchlist by organizing folders, filtering data, and sorting stocks based on key attributes
  • Create alerts based on custom price or indicator conditions
  • Receive daily email summaries for your watchlist and alerts
  • Create a personal account using email or Google Sign-In to securely manage your watchlist, alerts, and preferences

Data & Updates

RM Stock Tracker is designed to provide timely and reliable market data while maintaining performance and efficiency.

  • Intraday updates: Stock data is refreshed approximately every 30 minutes during active market hours, helping ensure that price movements and key metrics remain up to date throughout the trading day.
  • Market-aware scheduling: Updates are performed only during relevant market periods (market open and select extended-hours windows), avoiding unnecessary processing while keeping data accurate when it matters most.
  • Market open and close coverage: Additional updates are performed shortly after market open and again after market close to capture accurate daily pricing and summary data.
  • Evening refinement: A final update is performed in the evening to ensure closing prices, volume, and end-of-day metrics are fully reflected.
  • Market status: The market status (open, closed, or extended-hours) is updated frequently to reflect near real-time conditions across the platform.
  • Data transparency: Market status and last updated timestamps are displayed across the platform to provide clear visibility into current market conditions and data freshness.

Note: Market data may be subject to slight delays depending on the data provider.

Market Data

RM Stock Tracker uses market data from trusted third-party providers, including Massive (formerly known as Polygon).

Constituents data for major market indices is sourced from publicly available datasets, including Wikipedia.

Market data is provided for informational purposes only and may be subject to delays or adjustments. RM Stock Tracker is not intended to provide financial or investment advice.

Built With Care

The app is designed with performance, clarity, and usability in mind. It follows modern web development best practices and is continuously improved based on real usage.

The platform also supports both light and dark mode themes, allowing users to choose a viewing experience that suits their preference. The theme can be toggled easily from the navigation bar.
